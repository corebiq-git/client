2. GST AUTHORISATION

The Authorised Partner is hereby authorised to represent the Firm before the Central/State Goods and Services Tax authorities, GST Common Portal, Appellate authorities, and other government departments in matters directly connected with GST compliance.

3. GST REGISTRATION & COMPLIANCE

The Authorised Partner is authorised to apply for GST registration, submit amendments, manage authorised signatories, and undertake GST compliance activities including filing GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9, managing e-way bills, e-invoicing, ITC reconciliation, and processing refund applications.

4. GST PORTAL AUTHORISATION

The Authorised Partner is authorised to operate the Firm's GST portal account, use portal credentials, digital signature, EVC, or Aadhaar authentication only for legitimate Firm-related purposes.

5. REPRESENTATION & PAYMENTS

The Authorised Partner may sign, execute, and submit documents, respond to notices, attend proceedings, and coordinate payment of GST liabilities (tax, interest, penalties) from the Firm's funds.

6. LIMITATION OF AUTHORITY

This Agreement authorises the Authorised Partner for GST-related matters only. It does not authorise the Partner to sell assets, borrow money, alter bank accounts, or amend the Partnership Deed.

7. VALIDITY & REVOCATION

This authorisation shall remain valid from the date of execution until revoked in writing by the Partners. This Agreement shall be read together with the Partnership Deed of the Firm.
